Featured services

Premium Synthetic Engine Oil Change

We will remove your spent engine oil and filter. Then we will replace them with premium synthetic engine oil designed to be compatible with your engine and a premium quality oil filter. We will also reset your vehicle’s onboard engine oil life monitor (if equipped) or provide an oil change sticker.

During most visits to Tate’s Motorcar Ltd. you will enjoy the following complimentary services.

We will

  • Wash your front floor mats
  • Vacuum your front floor area
  • Top up your washer fluid
  • Correct your tire pressures
  • Clean your engine area and battery
  • Tire shine your tires

Diesel Exhaust Filter Cleaning Service

We will remove soot/ash buildup from the diesel particulate filter using the Liqui Moly DPF wash system to restore exhaust flow, reduce regeneration frequency, improve performance, prevent DPF-related warning lights, avoid limp mode and save expensive DPF replacement.

System

Windshield Repair

We will fix small chips and cracks by injecting resin and curing it to strengthen the glass, improve visibility, and help prevent the damage from spreading (often avoiding a full windshield replacement). If windshield replacement is unavoidable, we will replace your windshield with professional techniques at a fair and reasonable price.

Options

  • Chip and crack resin repair
  • Full windshield replacement when needed

Premium Dripless Oil Spray (Rust Protection)

We will apply a corrosion-inhibiting dripless oil based coating to rust-prone areas (frame, rocker panels, seams, inside cavities, etc). Dripless products are designed to cling with minimal runoff while still creeping into seams.

Wheel Alignment

We will adjust camber, caster, and toe so the vehicle tracks straight and tires wear evenly; helps with steering feel, stability, and fuel efficiency, especially after suspension work or curb/pothole impacts. Wheel alignment correction can save expensive tires from premature wear.
